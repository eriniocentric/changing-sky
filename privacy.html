Changing Sky — Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Changing Sky is a community explorer of public alerts from the NSF–DOE Vera C. Rubin Observatory. It is not an official Rubin Observatory product.

What you can do without an account

Anyone can browse the sky, alerts, votes and comments without signing in. Browsing does not create an account or store personal information on our side.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in (via an emailed link or Google sign-in). If you use Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile (name, profile picture) with the sign-in service; we do not request access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your votes on alerts, and the optional reason you give.
  • Your comments and the display name you choose to show with them.

What is public

Vote counts, vote reasons, comments and the display names attached to comments are visible to everyone. Your email address is never shown publicly.

How the information is used

Only to run the site: to let you vote and comment, to rank alerts the community finds interesting, and to help moderators choose alerts for follow-up observations. We do not sell or share your information, show advertising, or use it for marketing.

Where it is stored

Accounts, votes and comments are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Sky images and alert data are loaded from public astronomy services (CDS Strasbourg and the Fink broker); those services may see your IP address as any website you load content from would.

Cookies and local storage

When you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in. If you post a comment, your chosen display name is remembered in your browser. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own comments and remove your own votes at any time on the site. To delete your account and everything linked to it, open a request on the project's GitHub issues page (do not post your email address publicly; we will arrange a private reply).

Moderation

Moderators may hide comments that are abusive, spam or off-topic.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
